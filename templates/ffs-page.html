Ffs Bear Trap Map

BT1, BT2, the planned BT3 and the HQ, with every assigned spot checked against where players actually are on MightPulse. Download the image and post it for the alliance.

Update the plan (ksmapper)
  1. Open your map in ksmapper and click ↑ Export.
  2. Click Copy to Clipboard.
  3. Paste below. The image redraws by itself.

Traps are found by label, so keep BT1, BT2 and BT3 in their names. The HQ is the one labelled with your alliance tag. Cities count when they sit within the distance below of either trap. Everything else in the plan is ignored.

Update positions (MightPulse)
  1. Open the kingdom 1130 map on MightPulse. If Last Location Update is old, click Update Map and wait until it finishes.
  2. Open the raw map data. In Firefox, switch to Raw Data first.
  3. Press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C, and paste below.

Only the positions are used. A spot counts as blocked when anyone else's city overlaps any of its four tiles.
